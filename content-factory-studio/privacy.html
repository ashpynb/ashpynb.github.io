Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What the app accesses

If you connect TikTok, the app receives your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar — scope user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos to your account as drafts (scope video.upload).

Where data is stored

Everything stays on your own computer: your videos, settings and the TikTok access token. The app has no server and we do not collect, sell or share your data with anyone.

What is sent to TikTok

Only the videos and captions you choose to upload, sent directly from your computer to TikTok's official API.

Removing your data

Disconnect TikTok in the app (this deletes the stored token) or revoke access in TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions). Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder removes all local data.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.
